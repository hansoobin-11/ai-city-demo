# AI CITY Demo Agent Guide

- Read `docs/specs/requirements.md` before implementation.
- Preserve the approved 1920×1080 visual direction and do not redesign adjacent screens without approval.
- Work in small reviewable phases. Stop at each acceptance gate.
- Do not invent product capabilities, customer claims, metrics, URLs, or credentials.
- Treat touch as a primary input. Hover may enhance mouse use but must never be required.
- Reproduce reported bugs before changing code and verify the same scenario after the fix.
- Do not claim completion without typecheck, build, browser inspection, and the relevant touch-screen checklist.
